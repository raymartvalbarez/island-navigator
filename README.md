# Island Navigator

Bago National High School ADM math game — Grade 11 General Mathematics, Week 9 (Nov 9–13, 2026): bearings, right-triangle trigonometry, Law of Sines, Law of Cosines, and area of oblique triangles. Sail your banca between islands around Roxas City / Capiz.

**Live:** https://raymartvalbarez.github.io/island-navigator/
**Demo (no scores, no try used):** https://raymartvalbarez.github.io/island-navigator/?demo=1
**Projector leaderboard:** https://raymartvalbarez.github.io/island-navigator/leaderboard.html

- 5 levels × 8 items, 3 lives per level, 35–45 s per item, 4 answer choices
- L1 Bearings & compass · L2 SOH-CAH-TOA · L3 Law of Sines (AAS/ASA, no ambiguous SSA) · L4 Law of Cosines (SAS/SSS) · L5 Mixed & area (½ab sin C, Heron)
- Points per correct: 10/15/20/25/30 by level + up to 5 time bonus (max 1000)
- 1 official try; learner picker from the ADM class list; scores in MantleDB namespace `bnhs-inavigator-nov2026`
- Wrong answers never reveal the correct option. Items are generated and self-verified in `questions.js`.
